# How Power Works

Every item's power is **fixed and predetermined**. There is no randomness — an item's name determines its stats forever.

---

## Rarity and Total Power

An item's rarity tier sets its total power budget:

| Tier | Total Power |
|------|------------|
| Common | 15 pts |
| Rare | 40 pts |
| Legendary | 90 pts |
| Ultimate | 180 pts |

This total is always the sum of that item's Curse + Doom + Hex.

---

## How the Power Splits

The power budget is distributed across three forces. The split is unique to each item and derived from its name. Some items are balanced (roughly equal across all three), others are specialists (one force very high, the others low).

**Common item (15 pts) examples:**
- Balanced: Curse 5 / Doom 5 / Hex 5
- Specialist: Curse 10 / Doom 3 / Hex 2

**Ultimate item (180 pts) examples:**
- Balanced: Curse 60 / Doom 61 / Hex 59
- Specialist: Curse 126 / Doom 27 / Hex 27

---

## Total Power

Your effigy's total power is the sum of all Curse + Doom + Hex across every equipped item.

With 6 item slots (body, blade, offhand, necklace, left eye, right eye), a typical full build lands between **200 and 800** total power. An all-Ultimate setup can push toward the theoretical maximum.

---

## Strategy

- **Chasing Total Power?** → Equip the highest rarity items you have
- **Targeting the Curse ranking?** → Prioritise items with a high Curse split
- **Doom or Hex rankings?** → Same logic

You can see each item's individual Curse / Doom / Hex values in the Equipped Items list on the right panel.
