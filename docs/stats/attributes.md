# Curse · Doom · Hex

The three dark forces that make up an effigy's power.

---

## Curse ☠

Active, directed malice. The force of harm, binding, and intent. Sharp-edged, deliberate, merciless objects carry high Curse.

**High Curse items:** Blades, claws, teeth, bone-carved weapons.

---

## Doom ☽

The weight of inevitability. It does not strike — it settles. The slow accumulation of fate, the certainty that something terrible is coming. Ancient, grave-touched objects that carry the memory of death hold high Doom.

**High Doom items:** Skulls, grave dirt bottles, ancient tablets, funeral objects.

---

## Hex ✦

Ambient dark energy — spreading like a stain, impossible to contain. A pure Hex object doesn't need to be wielded; its presence is enough. Crystals, orbs, sigils, and eyes carry this force.

**High Hex items:** Crystal shards, scrying stones, runic objects, eye motifs.

---

## Balance vs Specialisation

The three forces are not rivals — all three contribute to your Total Power. But the Ritual Rankings have **separate leaderboards** for each force, which opens up different collector strategies:

- **The Curse Witch** — razor-sharp build, high Curse, destructive and precise
- **The Doom Bearer** — heavy and inevitable, Doom-focused build
- **The Hex Weaver** — diffuse and everywhere, Hex-optimised

---

## Where to See Stats

Each equipped item's Curse / Doom / Hex values are shown in the right panel list. The three circles at the bottom show your running totals.
