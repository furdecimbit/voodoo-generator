# Hex & Stitch

Hex & Stitch is a dark-arts voodoo doll generator. Use artefacts from your collection to build your own **Ritual Effigy**, bind it to your soul, and claim your place among the most powerful witches in the Rankings.

---

## What Can You Do?

- Choose from hundreds of artefacts to build your voodoo doll
- Open packs to earn new and rare items
- Save your build to your Bound Soul slot
- Compete in the Ritual Rankings by total dark power

---

## Where to Start

Connect your wallet, open your packs, forge your effigy.
