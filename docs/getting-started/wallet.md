# Connect Your Wallet

Connecting your wallet lets you see your packs, sync your inventory to the cloud, and appear in the Rankings.

---

## Solana

Phantom, Backpack, Solflare, and other injected Solana wallets are supported.

1. Click **Connect** on the Solana row in the left panel
2. If multiple wallets are detected, a picker appears — choose your preferred one
3. Approve the connection in your wallet extension
4. Your address appears truncated (e.g. `3xKz…m9Pq`)

Once connected, the app automatically finds your Hex & Stitch packs and loads your inventory.

---

## Tezos

Temple, Kukai, and Naan wallets are supported via the Beacon protocol.

1. Click **Connect** on the Tezos row
2. Approve the pairing request in your wallet
3. Your `tz1…` address appears

---

## What Connecting Unlocks

| Feature | Without Wallet | With Wallet |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Build & randomize | ✓ | ✓ |
| Open packs | ✗ | ✓ |
| Cloud inventory sync | ✗ | ✓ |
| Save Bound Soul | ✗ | ✓ |
| Appear in Rankings | ✗ | ✓ |

---

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** on the relevant row. Your local data is not affected.
