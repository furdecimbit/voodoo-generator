# Your First Ritual

Build your first voodoo doll step by step.

---

## 1. Pick a Body

Open the **Body** section in the left panel and click any texture — the preview updates instantly. The body is the foundation of your effigy. You can adjust its colour with the sliders at any time.

---

## 2. Equip Your Artefacts

Work through the remaining sections:

| Section | What You're Equipping |
|---------|-----------------------|
| **Blade** | The weapon in your doll's hand |
| **Offhand** | Other hand — potions, idols, relics |
| **Necklace** | A talisman at the throat |
| **Eyes** | Left and right eye — can be different |
| **Mouth** | Stitched or open expression |

Click any item to stage it. A confirmation bar slides in at the bottom of the preview. Click another item to replace it.

---

## 3. Adjust Colours

Two sets of sliders sit above the preview:

- **Overlay** — shifts the colour tint of the effigy's aura and clothing tones
- **Body** — adjusts the hue and saturation of the body texture

Click the colour boxes next to each slider to enter a hex code directly.

---

## 4. Randomize (Optional)

Click the **dice icon** in the menu bar — every slot fills with a random item. Click again to re-roll. Let the ritual decide.

---

## 5. Bewitch

When you're ready, click **Bewitch**. The ritual circle spins, a veil animation plays, and your effigy is fully rendered.

> Bewitch is only active when you have staged (not yet committed) changes.

---

## 6. Save to Bound Soul

Click the **floppy disk icon** next to the Bound Soul slot at the bottom of the right panel. Your build is saved — and synced to the cloud if your wallet is connected.

---

## 7. Download

Click the **↓ icon** in the menu bar to save your effigy as a full-resolution PNG.
