# Ritual Effigy Forge

The Forge is the main interface — where everything comes together.

---

## Left Panel — Item Selector

All item categories are listed as expandable accordion sections. Click a header to open or close it.

**Rarity filter:** Each section has a filter bar at the top — Common / Rare / Legendary / Ultimate. Click one to show only that tier. Click again to reset.

**Rarity glow:** Item borders indicate rarity at a glance:
- Grey border → Common
- Gold glow → Rare
- Cyan glow → Legendary
- Pink glow → Ultimate

---

## Centre — Live Preview

Your effigy updates in real time with every item selection or slider change.

**Menu bar** (above the preview):
- Dice icon → Randomize all slots
- ↓ icon → Download as PNG
- Ranks → Open the Ritual Rankings

---

## Right Panel — Equipped Items

A scrollable list of everything currently equipped. Each row shows:
- Item image and name
- Category and rarity label
- Individual Curse / Doom / Hex values
- ✕ button to unequip

Directly below the list, the **Total Power** circles show your running Curse, Doom, and Hex totals — updated live.

At the very bottom, the **Bound Soul** save slot.

---

## Music & Sound

The music bar at the top of the left panel:
- ◀ / ▶ to change track
- ▶ / ⏸ to play or pause
- SFX button to toggle sound effects on or off
