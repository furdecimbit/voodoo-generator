# Pack System

Packs are NFTs on the Solana blockchain. Every pack is **single-use** — once opened, it cannot be opened again.

---

## Pack Tiers & Drops

| Pack | Items Received | Contents | Bonus Chance |
|------|---------------|----------|-------------|
| **Common** | 5 items | 5× Common | 10% — one item becomes Rare |
| **Rare** | 3 items | 2× Rare + 1× Common | 10% — one Rare becomes Legendary |
| **Legendary** | 4 items | 2× Legendary + 1× Rare + 1× Common | 10% — one Legendary becomes Ultimate |
| **Ultimate** | 5 items | 2× Ultimate + 1× Legendary + 1× Rare + 1× Common | No upgrade — already the peak |

Every pack drops items from **different categories** — you won't get two blades from one pack.

---

## How to Open a Pack

1. Connect your Solana wallet
2. Your packs appear in the **Pack Inventory** section of the left panel
3. Click **Open** on the pack you want to open
4. Watch the ritual opening animation
5. Your items are revealed one by one on the drop screen
6. Click **Claim** to confirm — items are added to your inventory

---

## One-Time Use

If you attempt to open the same pack twice, the app will reject it. This check happens on the server — clearing local storage will not bypass it.

---

## Inventory Storage

Your items are stored in two places:

- **Cloud (Wix)** — tied to your wallet, synced across devices
- **Local** — offline fallback

When your wallet connects, both sources are merged. Cloud always takes priority.
